# Grove Monument Design — Cancelled and Archived

**As of:** 2026-07-22.

**Status:** **Cancelled.** The Grove monument experiment is closed. The Grove stays **monument-free**; no bust, no plinth, no medallion in the roundabout.

The author has replaced the original Marble Bust + Mask Rond design and the Subsequent Bronze Portrait and Oil Medallion redesign with the resolution below.

## Final resolution

- The Grove’s gravel roundabout is held **empty**: no statue, no portrait, no plinth, no mask inside the Grove.
- The masks live elsewhere in the gallery, behind the cloakroom wall (not in the Grove).
- All earlier bronze portrait and oil medallion iteration, including the Blender-authored `assets/models/grove-bronze-portrait.glb` and the `tools/blender/*.html` test rig, is **discarded**.
- Future redesigns of the Grove may proceed only under a new spec, not under the previous designs.
